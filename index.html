Privacy Policy

Diamond & Lapidary Calculator Pro

Effective date: September 13, 2026

Privacy at a glance

Diamond & Lapidary Calculator Pro is an offline application. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information through the app.

Information stored on your device

The app allows you to create and store information such as:

  • Gemstone inventory, measurements, costs, values, notes, and favorites
  • Customer names and cutting-project details entered by you
  • Custom gemstone profiles and calculation inputs
  • App settings, including appearance preferences

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

Stone photographs

You may choose a stone photograph using Android's system document picker. The app stores permission to display the selected image. It does not upload the photograph to the developer.

Reports and exports

You may create PDF reports and CSV exports. Android lets you choose where these files are saved. After export, you control the file and any services or people with whom you share it. Third-party storage or sharing services have their own privacy practices.

Permissions and network access

The app does not request general internet access, location, contacts, microphone, advertising, or Google Play Billing permissions. Access to a photograph is limited to files you select through Android's document picker.

Payments

This is a fully unlocked paid Pro app with no advertisements, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Google Play processes the initial app purchase and download under Google's own privacy policy. The app and developer do not receive your complete payment-card information.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system or your device provider may include app data in a device backup according to your account and device settings. Those backup services are controlled by their respective providers, not by the developer.

Data retention and deletion

App records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files remain in the locations where you saved or shared them and must be deleted separately.

Children's privacy

The app is a professional calculation and inventory tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Local storage reduces transmission risk, but no device or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and use care when exporting records containing customer information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or legal requirements change. A revised policy will display a new effective date on this page.
